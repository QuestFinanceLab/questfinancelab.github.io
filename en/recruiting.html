Recruiting

We welcome students who are genuinely interested in finance and prepared to participate consistently in regular sessions and collaborative projects.

Quest places greater emphasis on curiosity, commitment, and collaboration than on a particular major or prior financial knowledge.

Members study financial theory and analytical methods in regular sessions, then apply them to real-world markets and companies through practical exercises and company research projects.

Who We Look For

01

Interest in Finance

Students who are genuinely interested in financial markets and companies and want to continue learning about them.

02

Commitment to Learning

Students who are willing to learn new financial concepts and analytical methods and apply them in practice.

03

Responsibility

Students who can participate consistently and responsibly in regular sessions and team projects.

04

Collaboration

Students who can explain their analysis and views with evidence and engage constructively with other members.
