About

We seek to understand finance, analyze it, and articulate our own perspective.

Quest is an intercollegiate finance and investment society dedicated to analyzing financial markets and companies while systematically developing practical analytical skills.

Our curriculum is grounded in accounting, corporate finance, macroeconomics, and investment theory, and extends to valuation, equities, fixed income, derivatives, and risk management. We aim to connect financial theory with real-world market and company analysis.

Through regular sessions and applied workshops, members analyze financial data, build evidence-based views on companies and markets, and develop their findings into research reports and other concrete outputs.

Quest values intellectual curiosity and a genuine interest in finance over any particular academic background or prior experience. We learn and analyze together, with the goal of becoming finance professionals who can explain their own views with clarity and logic.

Our Philosophy

Quest is built on the idea that individual growth should accumulate into organizational capability.

Rather than pursuing scale for its own sake, we focus on the learning and experience of each member and on creating an environment in which everyone can meaningfully deepen their financial knowledge and analytical skills.

The knowledge and experience developed within Quest are not meant to remain with individuals. As members grow, they become mentors and project leaders for the next cohort, sharing what they have learned and supporting the development of newer members.

Learning becomes experience, and experience becomes the next person's starting point.
Through this cycle of accumulation and transmission, Quest seeks to build an organization that grows sustainably over time.
