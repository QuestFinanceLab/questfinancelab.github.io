Curriculum

We connect financial theory and data-driven practice with company research projects.

Quest's curriculum is designed to develop the ability to apply financial theory to real-world data and company analysis.

Regular sessions cover accounting, macroeconomics, corporate finance, investment theory, derivatives, risk management, and other core areas of finance, alongside hands-on work using Excel, Python, and DART.

Members apply what they learn to company analysis and research-report writing, developing their findings into final team company reports and stock pitches.

Regular activities are held every Friday from 19:00 to 22:00.

Regular Activities

01

Theory Session

We study the core concepts in accounting, macroeconomics, corporate finance, and investment theory required for financial analysis.

02

Practice Session

We apply theoretical concepts to real-world data using DART, Excel, Python, and related tools.

03

Company Research Project

Teams prepare company research reports and present their findings in a final stock pitch.

04

Research Reports

Beyond the regular project, members continue to produce research on companies and financial markets.

05

Asset Management

An optional portfolio management program available to members from the second cohort onward.
